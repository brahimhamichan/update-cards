# Community guidelines

Everyone is welcome to participate. Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions. Focus criticism on the work, give people room to learn, and respect differing experience and perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable. Keep examples free of personal data and credentials.

Report concerns privately to [Brahim](mailto:brahim@scalingadventures.com). Reports will be handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

These guidelines apply in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
